About the project

Purpose

Predict a student's academic performance category and suggest targeted improvements, helping students recognise weaknesses and helping teachers understand class-level patterns.

Technology stack
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js, Font Awesome
  • Backend: Python, Flask, REST-style JSON APIs
  • Machine learning: scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy
  • Database: SQLite with SQLAlchemy
Algorithms compared

Decision Tree – interpretable rule-like splits.

Logistic Regression – linear probabilistic model (features are standardised).

Random Forest – ensemble of trees that reduces overfitting.

Important limitations
  • The training dataset is synthetic and does not represent real students.
  • Training labels come from a documented demonstration scoring rule (plus random noise), not real outcomes.
  • Replace or validate these rules with historical results before any real deployment.
  • Probabilities are model estimates, not guarantees of success.
  • Recommendations reflect recorded academic factors only; they make no claim about ability, personality or future.
Future work
  • Train on real, anonymised institutional data.
  • Add login roles for students and teachers.
  • Plug a language model into the modular recommender.
  • Add subject-wise marks and early-warning alerts.
